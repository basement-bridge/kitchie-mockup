Clear by tapping the lit icon again

Baseline is option B. The list opens with no Location or Category filter, titled “All items”. The clock is its own on/off button (outline when off, filled when on) that sorts by use by; it works with or without Location or Category. Pin and tag are only for choosing a grouping: once one is picked, the ribbon takes over as it does now.

Pin and tag are two buttons that can each be on or off, like the clock. Tap the lit one and it turns off: the ribbon goes and the list is the plain A to Z list again.

Costs: No new control and no new row. The only thing to learn is that a lit icon turns off. Nothing on screen says so, so first-timers may not find it; the tooltip and screen reader label say “tap again to clear”.
