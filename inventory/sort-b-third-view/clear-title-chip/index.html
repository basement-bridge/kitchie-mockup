Clear with an ✕ on the title

Baseline is option B. The list opens with no Location or Category filter, titled “All items”. The clock is its own on/off button (outline when off, filled when on) that sorts by use by; it works with or without Location or Category. Pin and tag are only for choosing a grouping: once one is picked, the ribbon takes over as it does now.

When Location or Category is on, the title becomes a chip with an ✕ (“Location ✕”). Tap the ✕ and the view clears. Pin and tag stay a plain two-way switch for changing between the two.

Costs: The ✕ is visible and sits where you read the state. The title chip is wider than the plain title, and it is the only place a clear lives, so it moves when the title changes.
