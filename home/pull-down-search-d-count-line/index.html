Try: pull the list down from the top and type milk, cheese or e. Also: swipe a row, tap the − or tick, the plus to add, the pin and tag toggles, the clock (Use by) and the ribbon. Undo appears for 5 seconds after a change.

  • Pull down from the top to search. Closing the field (Cancel, the Search key, a tap outside, scrolling) keeps the search on.
  • The quietest version: no strip. Under the title a small line says 3 of 13 match “milk” with a ×. Tap the words to edit the search.
  • The words that matched are marked inside each row (the name and its second line), so you can see why it is there.
  • A search is temporary: it is cleared by the × or by pulling down for a new one; it never changes your Location or Category.
  • Not remembered: a refresh or leaving the screen clears it (a search is temporary). The live app today remembers the last search unless Settings turns that off; keeping or dropping that is still to be decided.
