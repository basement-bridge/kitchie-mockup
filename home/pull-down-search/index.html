Theme

Try: pull the list down from the top to search. Tap the chip to edit, × to clear. Also: swipe a row right (use one) or left (used up), tap the − or tick on a row, the plus to add, the pin and tag switch (Location or Category), the ribbon (scroll it; press and hold a chip to reorder) and Sort. Undo appears for 5 seconds after a change.

  • Pull down from the top: the search field slides in at the top of the list. Let go past the line to open it, short of it to spring back.
  • Search is one more filter. It narrows the list live on top of the Location or Category tab and the sort, and never resets them.
  • Close the field with Cancel, the Search key, a tap outside, or by scrolling down. Closing keeps the search as a chip in the ribbon row.
  • Tap the chip to edit that search (field pre-filled). Tap its × to clear it. Neither touches Location or Category.
  • Pulling down again always starts a new, empty search and drops the old one. Cancel on an empty field leaves no chip.
  • Costs: no icon, no search row until you pull; one chip only while a search is active.
