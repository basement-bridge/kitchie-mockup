What to look at

  • Short of the line the spring rests at full height. Past the line it is squashed down with the lens glowing, then it releases with a bounce.
  • Motion: squash, quiver, boing, settle, every 2.2 s. Transforms only.
  • The magnifier is the app's own search symbol, so the pill still reads as search.
  • Reduced motion: nothing moves. The armed pill shows the held pose (bands taut, door ajar, lid lifted, spring squashed, egg cracked) with the same words.
