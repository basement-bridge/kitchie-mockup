What to look at

  • Short of the line the door is shut. Past the line it opens to a crack, the light flickers in the gap and the whole fridge shudders.
  • Motion: the door swings between open and shut, with a rattle before it, every 2.4 s. Transforms and opacity only.
  • The fridge is drawn in the app's sage line style, and the light uses the theme's gold.
  • Reduced motion: nothing moves. The armed pill shows the held pose (bands taut, door ajar, lid lifted, spring squashed, egg cracked) with the same words.
