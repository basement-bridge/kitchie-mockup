What to look at

  • Short of the line the bands sag slack and the pill rests with its down arrow. Past the line the bands pull straight and the arrow becomes the magnifier.
  • Motion: the pill sinks about 7 px, trembles, then recoils and settles, every 1.8 s. Only transforms.
  • Why it works: stretched band means let go and it will spring, which is what releasing does. The pill sits where it does today.
  • Reduced motion: nothing moves. The armed pill shows the held pose (bands taut, door ajar, lid lifted, spring squashed, egg cracked) with the same words.
