  • Home screen

    The Kitchen list as the app shows it: a Use by clock (on at every load: every item, soonest first, then items with no date), pin and tag toggles that bring out a Location or Category ribbon (it hides itself, scrolls and reorders by long press), search by typing, pulling down or voice, with a reason and a way out when nothing matches, swipe to use one or mark used up, add an item, Undo and the shopping list. Switch between a full kitchen, an empty one and the read-only view.

  • Item detail: read view

    The item screen as a read view with field-level editing, no tabs and no Save button. Brand and Back share the top row; long names shrink to two lines then drag sideways; quantity and unit read as one human-friendly value (1½ kg, 250 g) and are edited in place or in a sheet. More sections arrive step by step.
