Our bold claims on privacy →
We just use Google to sign you in. We don’t know your name. We don’t know your email. You get a made-up name, and you can change it any time. The old one isn’t kept. No trackers, no ads, no third-party scripts. Nothing on the page phones anyone else. Invite links work once and expire after 48 hours. Your kitchen starts empty. Only the people you invite will see it. Jane won’t be a member and can’t see your items. You’ll share items and the shopping list with the other members of Casa Jane.
