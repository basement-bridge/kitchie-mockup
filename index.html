Straight answers

The questions you’re probably asking.

How do you make money?

Right now, we don’t. Kitchie is an idea we’re building and testing, and you’re helping shape it.

How will you pay for it later?

We don’t know yet. But not by selling your data or showing you ads. We dislike those ideas as much as you do.

If Kitchie turns out to be a genuinely useful tool, we’ll look at donations, a subscription, or a free version with optional premium features.

How your privacy works
Only an anonymous ID from Google is used, to recognise you next time. Your email address is not requested and is not kept. Your name is not requested from Google either. You start with a playful made-up one and can change it to anything on the Household page. If you change it, the old name is not kept: there is no name history.
