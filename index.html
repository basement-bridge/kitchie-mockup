  • Kitchen list: Use by first

    The agreed filter row for the Kitchen list: Use by is on at every load and shows what expires within a month (from the cache, then refreshed, then the rest in the background); pin and tag are off until chosen and turn off when tapped again; the ribbon hides itself 3 seconds after a pick and 5 seconds when idle, and the title reads the pick. The other sort options stay as variations under Kitchen list.

  • Home screen

    The Kitchen list you can use: a pin and tag switch that turns the tab ribbon into Location or Category (the ribbon scrolls and reorders by long press, and remembers its order), sort, search by typing or voice, swipe to use one or mark used up, add an item, Undo and the shopping list. Switch between a full kitchen, an empty one and the read-only view.

    Production

  • Item detail: read view

    The item screen as a read view with field-level editing, no tabs and no Save button. Brand and Back share the top row; long names shrink to two lines then drag sideways; quantity and unit read as one human-friendly value (1½ kg, 250 g) and are edited in place or in a sheet. More sections arrive step by step.

    Draft

  • Add and edit item

    The one form for adding and editing an item: the name as the heading, Details and Stock & more tabs, a quantity stepper with a unit, Location and Spot suggestions, and a use-by date you can add and clear. Switch between Add and Edit, and jump to each state.
