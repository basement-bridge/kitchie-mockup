Draft concept (Kitchie #192, with #191)

Stock check

  • Amounts drift once things are used. Nobody takes a fraction off the butter each time, so the app asks at quiet moments instead of pretending to know.
  • Only where you already are. Never a push, never a timer.
  • Your words stay as you typed them. An estimate is labelled and never shown as fact.
  • The assistant can do the thinking. It knows eggs go off and a freezer slows that down, and stores the estimate so nobody has to keep state.
  • Always a way out: stop for one item, one area or category, or everything. Stopping the questions keeps the estimate.
