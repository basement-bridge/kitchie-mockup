One ribbon, two views. Pin means location, tag means category. Whichever is in front decides what the ribbon filters, and switching starts again from All.

The thumb slides to show which view is in front, so both states are visible at once.

Option 2: one button

The button shows the view you are in. Tap and it flips to the other.
